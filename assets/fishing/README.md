# Turbo Reel assets

- `sprites/`: one PNG per sprite (`<id>.png`, its animation frames side by side), `sprites.json` (frame size, frame count, anchor per sprite) and the `.import` files Godot writes.
- `art.json`: the palette (`FishingPalette.ALL`) and the sprite list in the art pipeline's format, written by the same tool.
- `font/turbo_reel_5x7.png`: the HUD's pixel font, a glyph grid written from `src/games/fishing/tools/fishing_font_grids.gd` (`FishingFontGrids`: 5 x 7 capitals and digits, lower case with 2-row descenders, the HUD's punctuation, the en dash) and imported by Godot's Image Font importer (`turbo_reel_5x7.png.import`: 16 x 6 cells of 6 x 9, ascent 7, descent 2, integer scaling). Turbo Reel's HUD and, through `HudSpec.palette` (`text_px` 27, `key_px` 54, `grid_px` 3), the kit widgets draw it at one scale on the art's pixel grid (borderless panels in one exact colour, the deep teal, lighter than the boats; bars, meters and markers in whole art pixels), a glyph pixel 3 screen px (6 for key numbers), with no outline; `FishingHudDraw.font()` hangs every glyph from the baseline by the cap height (the importer hangs it by half the cell) and narrows the space to 3 px. A text line's box is 2 rows of leading, the 7-row cap and the 2-row descender, so the cap sits in its middle.
- `art_manifest.json`: the art pipeline's spend record ($3.56 of the $10 budget).

## Hand-authored pixel art

Every sprite is a palette-indexed text grid in `src/games/fishing/tools/fishing_art_grids.gd` (`FishingArtGrids`: one character per palette colour, `.` clear), plus the frames derived in code (the fish's swim, pull and tired poses, the cards composed from other sprites). Edit the grids, then write the PNGs and re-import:

```bash
. tools/env.sh
tools/godot.sh --headless --path . -s src/games/fishing/tools/fishing_write_sprites.gd -- --sheet=/abs/path/sheet.png
tools/godot.sh --headless --path . --import
```

`--sheet` also writes a 4x preview of every sprite and the assembled boat (driving and fishing). PNGs no sprite owns are deleted. `tests/games/fishing/art/` fails when a PNG differs from the grids, uses a colour outside the palette, breaks the style rules (a cool hue family, nothing darker than the abyss, no fish outlines or lone pixels) or the shapes the view relies on (fish face left with the mouth on the anchor in every pose, the pulls hang the tail lower, boat parts inside the hull canvas, a slate hull under a straight foam rail).

## Style

Modelled on Slynyrd's "Nemk": one cool hue family in eight value steps from foam white to abyss teal (`FishingPalette.LADDER`, nothing darker than `#1c4d65`), seabed sage, two slates for wood and hulls, one warm accent (`#c98a5e`, the brass wheel, lever knob, rod and the lighthouse bands) and three desaturated player colours that appear only on the rider's cap and the bow pennant. Forms are 2-3 flat top-lit planes with no outlines, no anti-aliasing, no dither and no texture; edges are horizontal and vertical, diagonals only in even 1:1 and 2:1 runs; structure is 2 px, 1 px only for straight accents. Nothing is ever rotated: the signed trim picks one of eight hull poses (`FishingRig.POSE_LEVELS`: the hull cut into bands moved by whole pixels; five bow up, the bow up to 8 px and the stern squatting up to 2 px, and two braking, the bow digging in 4 px and the stern lifting 2 px), every part riding its band; the rider leans into a sprint and sits back to brake. The boat's water is chunky whitewater lumps (`fishing_whitewater.gd`, mockups E4 and E7: a lit foam top over a sky face, a surface-teal seam, corners cut): a mound behind the transom that grows from cruising to the squatting hump, a rooster tail on the plane that breaks up and falls, the centre wake and the far V arm streaming back on the boat's odometer, the prop wash under the surface, the bow wave or forefoot spray, a mound against the transom and a reverse wake while a fish tows the boat, a mound ahead of the bow when braking. The outboard has a long-shaft leg: the prop stays in the water in every pose and heave. The water is light: a 2 px foam line at the surface (the only white band), then haze from the surface to 15 m, surface teal to 120 m, upper teal to 500 m and mid teal below, the same depths in every zone; the floor (`fishing_floor.gd`) is flat terraces with sheer drops, stepped boulders, a back ridge a step lighter than it and each zone's props (mooring blocks, anchors and crates in the harbour; seagrass, shells and starfish in the shallows; fan, brain and branching coral, anemones and small arches on the reef; kelp and ledges on the shelf; bones and caves in the deep; basalt columns before the abyss), all seeded by world cell, with the map's landmarks as set pieces (the harbour lighthouse, the great reef arch, the whale's bones, the old wreck); terraces are cut into raised and notched pieces, every drop has a dark side plane under a lit lip, and the props come in seeded clusters (`fishing_props.gd`); it is two steps darker than the water above it, sage in the shallows. Fish (D2: a dark back plane over the flank and a light belly, fins as their own shapes, an eye with a foam highlight) shift along the ladder with depth (`FishingPaletteMap`) and take their pose from the line: the swim cycle, a pull at 15 or 30 degrees (whole-pixel column shifts toward the mouth) or tired. The line is a small verlet rope (`fishing_rope.gd`: sagging with slack, straight under strain, more drag in the water than in the air) drawn as one Catmull-Rom curve thinned to 1 px (a straight line when taut), slate above the water and deep teal under it; the solid brass rod, thick at the butt, has fixed bent frames. The sky, clouds, islands, water bands, floor, dock, wakes and the world-edge waterfall are code-drawn from the same palette (`src/games/fishing/view/fishing_scenery.gd`, `fishing_view.gd`).

`tests/games/fishing/art/style_check.mjs` measures the rubric's numbers on screenshots at 3 screen px per art px (any art size: 1920x1080 for 640x360) with the HUD masked (its rects from `FishingHud.layout()`, framed panels wherever they are, `--hud=x,y,w,h` for the speed behind the stern): `node tests/games/fishing/art/style_check.mjs build/fishing/style/*.png`. The heart in the heart rate box (`#b5675f`) is the HUD's one colour outside the palette.

Sprites: `boat_hull` (96 x 40, the long-shaft leg included; level, five bow-up and two braking poses; boat-local points in `view/fishing_rig.gd`), `boat_crew` (clutch lever, tiller, rider: driving, fishing, leaning in, sitting back; the flywheel ring, crank and leg are drawn by `FishingBoatView` at the exact crank angle), `boat_winch`, `boat_prop`, `lure`, ten `fish_<species>` with six frames (swim 1-3, pull 15, pull 30, tired), seven `icon_*` minimap and leaderboard icons (foam pixels take the player colour), `scenery_town`, `scenery_pier`, `gull`, `fx_splash`, `fx_sparkle`, and the cards `picker_card` (the game's picker image), `card_roam` and `card_workout` (160 x 90).
