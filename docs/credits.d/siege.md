# Credits: siege

Third-party assets (art, audio, fonts) the siege game ships, with their licenses and sources.

- None. Every image is drawn by Siege's own CPU rasterizer and every sound is synthesized at runtime with the platform's `Sfx.synth` (ADR "Synthesized audio" in `docs/decisions/siege.md`).
- Audio: no sound files and no third-party samples. The treadwheel creak, junction clatter, tar pour, bolt thunk, Scorpion, onager release and impact, arrow volley, hooves, ram, gate and horn are generated by `Sfx.synth` in `src/games/siege/audio/siege_audio.gd`; the coin and the Breach boom are the platform's own built-in sounds.
- Art: no generated or downloaded art (no fal calls). `assets/siege/bake/` (the world, its depth and the sprite atlases) is rendered by our own rasterizer, `src/games/siege/art/*.mjs` (`node src/games/siege/art/bake.mjs`); the HUD shapes are code.
- Fonts, UI kit and 3D kit: the platform's (`addons/wattlands/ui`, `addons/wattlands/world3d`), credited in the platform's credits. Siege adds none.
