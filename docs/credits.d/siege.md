# Credits: siege

Third-party assets (art, audio, fonts) the siege game ships, with their licenses and sources.

- None. Every mesh is procedural (`LowPolyMesh`, `MultiMeshScatter`) and every sound is synthesized at runtime with the platform's `Sfx.synth` (ADR "Synthesized audio" in `docs/decisions/siege.md`).
- Audio: no sound files and no third-party samples. The wheel creak, generator hum, tower shots, hooves, ram, gate and horn are generated by `Sfx.synth` in `src/games/siege/audio/siege_audio.gd`; the coin and the Breach boom are the platform's own built-in sounds.
- Art: no generated or downloaded art (no fal calls, no `assets/siege/` images). The miniature, the fort, the units and the HUD shapes are code.
- Fonts, UI kit and 3D kit: the platform's (`addons/wattlands/ui`, `addons/wattlands/world3d`), credited in the platform's credits. Siege adds none.
