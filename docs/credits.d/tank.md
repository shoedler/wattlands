# Credits: tank

Third-party assets (art, audio, fonts) the tank game ships, with their licenses and sources.

- Art, generated: the 61 sprite PNGs of `assets/tank/sprites/` (hulls, turrets, rooms, machinery, enemies, the Behemoth, props, effects, backdrops, the picker card) were generated with OpenAI's GPT Image 2.5 through fal (fal.ai) by the art pipeline (`tools/art/generate.mjs`, final endpoint `openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/text-to-image`, style-check drafts on `openai/gpt-image-2.5/flare/text-to-image`) from the original prompts in `assets/tank/art.json`, then pixelated to the 32-colour palette by `tools/art/pixelate.gd`. No person, text, insignia or flag is depicted. Spend, from `assets/tank/art_manifest.json`: 125 paid requests, $8.0438 of the $10 cap (44 flare drafts for $2.3738 over three style rounds; 81 sunburst requests for $5.67: the full pass of 61 for $4.27, then 20 rerolls for $1.40). 61 of the 125 results are the selected ones the game ships.
- Art, procedural: six ids ignore their generated PNG and are drawn in code (`CODE_DRAWN` in `src/games/tank/view/placeholder_art.gd`): `bg_ground`, `prop_wire`, `prop_tank_traps` (`TankObstacleArt`), `gear_medium`, `steam_puff`, `smoke_puff`. The view also draws in code, from the palette colours only: the halt line's dragon's teeth and lamp, flywheels, gears, the drive sprocket and chain, the dynamo rotor, capacitor jars, shells and racks, the flyball regulator, wall lamps and badges, the plain stretches and trees of the near strips (`TankStripArt`), and every particle and light effect (`TankParticles`, `TankFx`, `TankLights`). Every sprite is also regraded at load (`TankMaterial`, `TankPlaceholderArt`) and the planes are blurred by `TankBlur`; the broken Behemoth bogie and turret are derived from the whole ones. Any id without a PNG falls back to a procedural placeholder (`TankPlaceholderArt`).
- Audio: no sound files and no third-party samples. Every sound (diesel rumble, dynamo whine, steam chuff and latch hiss, cannon boom, shell whistle, spent casing, explosions, track clank, lever clunk, hoist ratchet) is synthesized at runtime with the platform's `Sfx.synth` (`src/games/tank/audio/tank_audio.gd`). No CC0 downloads.
- Fonts, UI kit and 2D kit: the platform's (`addons/wattlands/ui`, `addons/wattlands/world2d`), credited in the platform's credits. Tank adds none.
