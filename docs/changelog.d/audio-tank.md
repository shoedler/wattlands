# Changelog: audio-tank

- Tank's sound on the audio kit: a diesel with three unequal cylinders, valves, a turbo by watts, a rasp that opens with load and a crackle on lift-off; a dynamo, a steam engine, an arc ladder, a belt feed and a rivet hammer, each locked 1:1 to its rider's crank; track links and squeak locked to the distance drawn; the gun, the casing, the shell's flight and a far boom delayed by range; an incoming whistle that ends at impact; a night forest of wind in the pines, crickets, frogs, an owl and a far battle.
