# Changelog: audio-tank

- The Lights station no longer pierces: its buzz and zaps are gone. It is now a low generator thrum that follows your cadence, a soft relay tock on each stroke of the ladder's arc, a second tock when you are well over band, a rare dull spark at high reach and the carbon lamp's faint hiss. Its level in the band the ear is most sensitive to, 2-8 kHz, falls from about -4 dB of its own total to about -19 dB, and a test now holds it there. Nobody has listened to it yet.
