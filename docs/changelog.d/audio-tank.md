# Changelog: audio-tank

- Tank's sound on the audio kit: a diesel with three unequal cylinders, valves, a turbo by watts, a rasp that opens with load and a crackle on lift-off; a dynamo, a steam engine, an arc ladder, a belt feed and a rivet hammer, each locked 1:1 to its rider's crank; track links and squeak locked to the distance drawn; the gun, the casing, the shell's flight and a far boom delayed by range; an incoming whistle that ends at impact; a night forest of wind in the pines, a cricket chorus, cricket trains, frogs, an owl and a far battle. TankView now drives this, in place of the placeholder sounds it had.
- Every seat as lead sits at one level, in the mix's range; a Repairs seat with a full hull answers the rider's legs; the dynamo and arc move instead of holding flat lines; the diesel's takes no longer line up into a bar; the frogs, the far battle and the creaking branch reach the rider; the reload and the near impacts vary like the gun; a main-gun round on a near threat no longer booms from far away.
- Near threats can be heard: a recon car or a tankette bursting from the bushes now comes with a small high engine over your machine before you have to look, revving as it dashes, idling down when it stops to fire, and fading as it flees. Its gun cracks and its rounds ring on the plate instead of sounding like a shell landing.
- Far enemies sound far: a far shot is a low thump after its range, and its shell is faint in the air until its last second. A far battle in the background stands down while a real enemy is on the far panel, so a thump in a fight is always a shot.
- Segment and boss banners no longer play the safety warning or duck the mix, so the warning means a real safety prompt.
- Track squeak, shell ejection, shell loading, valves, turbo whistle and crackle, and the rewards of a far hit or kill, now read over a hard ride on every seat, and the tread clanks under a tow.
