# Changelog: audio-tank

- Tank's sound on the audio kit: a diesel with three unequal cylinders, valves, a turbo by watts, a rasp that opens with load and a crackle on lift-off; a dynamo, a steam engine, an arc ladder, a belt feed and a rivet hammer, each locked 1:1 to its rider's crank; track links and squeak locked to the distance drawn; the gun, the casing, the shell's flight and a far boom delayed by range; an incoming whistle that ends at impact; a night forest of wind in the pines, a cricket chorus, cricket trains, frogs, an owl and a far battle. TankView now drives this: the old runtime synth is gone.
- Every seat as lead sits at one level, in the mix's range; a Repairs seat with a full hull answers the rider's legs; the dynamo and arc move instead of holding flat lines; the diesel's takes no longer line up into a bar; the frogs, the far battle and the creaking branch reach the rider; the reload and the near impacts vary like the gun; a main-gun round on a near threat no longer booms from far away.
