# Changelog: audio-fishing

- Turbo Reel has real sound, rendered from recipes: the pedal outboard putts once per foot, locked to the crank each boat draws, with valve ticks, brass gear ticks, exhaust crackle and a gear whistle that rises with your power; the reel ratchets six clicks a revolution; the line creaks and sings higher as the tension rises, the rod groans under a hard pull and the drag screams on a run.
- The sea is a bed with swell, foam and wind by speed, a harbour that fades as you leave it, hull slaps that come faster with speed, gulls over the dock and a rare far one at sea, a buoy bell, rigging ticks and a fish breaking the surface now and then.
- Each leap of a jumping fish lands with its own splash; a trophy gets a top note on its fanfare.
- Other riders' boats sit 10 dB under yours and fade out with distance, and so do their casts, turns and splashes; a catch and the bells still reach you from anywhere.
- The clutch lever clunks back to the prop after a catch, a snap or a thrown hook. Lifting off burbles once, and never once the crank has stopped. A pause stands the boat: the sea no longer slaps at the speed it stopped at.
- The engine and the swell repeat far less often, and a fish breaking the surface or the hull knocking can now be heard while you wait.
