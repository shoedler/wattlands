# Changelog: fishing

## v0.3.0

- The pedal boat is a small physics sim: it surges with every pedal stroke, squats over the hump, climbs onto the plane from about 70 % FTP (7 % faster, flatter, the prop always in the water) and brakes bow-down when you stop.
- A hooked fish tows the boat toward it; big fish (30 kg and up) jerk the line on their runs with a short +50 % FTP tug when haptics are on.
- The fight panel shows reel-in progress; the help overlay explains the HUD.
