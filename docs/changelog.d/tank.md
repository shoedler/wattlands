# Changelog: tank

For riders:
- Torque Company opens in a hangar now. The first 3 minutes the tank sits on a mechanic's stand with its tracks spinning in the air, under white ceiling lights. Every seat works, and the drive, aim and fire drills happen here. In the last 10 seconds the lights go dark, red beacons turn, a soft klaxon sounds, the tank comes down off the stand and the door opens.
- The next 2 minutes are a roll-out through the door. It never stops. Both parts are warm-up, at 65% of your FTP or less, and the first 5 minutes are the same as before. Your ride's laps read Hangar and Roll-out.
- Far wrecks no longer hang at a fixed distance: they close in as the tank drives, and drop out of the far view when you pass them.
- The Loader builds shells a little even when you ride under the power band, at a fifth of its normal speed, instead of not at all. Staying in band is still much faster, and it still counts toward the score.
- A soft, short klaxon sounds three times in the hangar's last 10 seconds.
