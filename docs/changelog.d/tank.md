# Changelog: tank

### For riders

- **Torque Company opens in a hangar.** For the first 3 minutes the tank sits on a mechanic's stand under white ceiling lights, its tracks spinning in the air as you pedal. Every seat works, and the drive, aim and fire drills happen here. In the last 10 seconds the lights go dark, red beacons turn, a soft klaxon sounds, the tank comes down off the stand and the door opens.
- **Then a 2 minute roll-out through the door.** It never stops. Both parts are warm-up, at 65% of your FTP or less, and your first 5 minutes are the same as before. Your ride's laps read Hangar and Roll-out.
- **Far wrecks close in as you drive.** They no longer hang at one distance: they slide toward you with the road and drop out of the far view when you pass them.
- **The Loader builds a little under the band.** Below the power band it now builds shells at a fifth of its normal speed instead of not at all. Staying in band is still much faster, and only that counts for the score and the glow.
- **The Behemoth in Ambush is easier to bring down.** Its three bogies and its turret have a little less armour: two shells break a bogie and five the turret, where it took three and six. Ashfall Road's Behemoth is unchanged.
