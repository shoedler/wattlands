- **Torque Company's Secondaries are machine guns.** Three times the rate of fire, 540 rounds a minute at 90 rpm and still tied to your cranks, at a quarter of the damage a round. Recon cars, tankettes and barricades are scaled to match, so they fall as fast as they did. The effort does not change. The tracers, the muzzle light, the belts and the rattle follow. With no target the feed only clacks over, and a crewmate's burst no longer drowns the other machines. No one has heard the rattle yet.
