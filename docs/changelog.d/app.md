### Pedal navigation, forms, Strava text (app)

- Pedal navigation is now opt-in (Settings, off by default): without it menus and in-game pick lists use the keyboard and controller only. With it on, menus show a live power graph with the surge threshold.
- The Profile page (and every form page) scrolls to its last entries.
- Strava activities are titled `WATTLANDS: <game> - <workout or mission>` and describe the game's stats, then moving time, distance, average and normalized power, cadence and work.
- The product name shown to players is WATTLANDS.
