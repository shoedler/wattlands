# Changelog: audio-siege

- Castellum Rotae's audio is redone on the platform's kit: a timber treadwheel locked 1:1 to each rider's cadence (pawl, footfall, creak, axle knock, groan), the walkers now stepping with the same crank; the mine and pump locked to their art; a ballista's twang, whistling arrows, a tumbling stone and its delayed landing, tar, horns, the ram, a walking tower, the Breach; a gusting hill wind with crows, banners, war drums, a far anvil and a lark. The wheel strains under hard effort, safety prompts read over the horns and volleys, a pause silences the battle, and a host and a client hear the same gate.
