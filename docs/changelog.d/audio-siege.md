- Castellum Rotae's sound, overhauled after the owner played it: every arrow, stone and bolt now has a release, a swoosh that lasts its whole flight and crosses the screen with it, and an impact by what it struck: flesh, wood, a shield, dirt or water. The wheel is a low timber rumble with soft footfalls, one axle knock per revolution and a quiet pawl, with no creak or whine and no pitch that follows your cadence. Hooves stop covering the battle. Measured in real sessions; no person has heard it yet.
- Your own station's shots now sit over everyone else's, a hit stands out of the flights, a killing blow sounds like a hit and not a miss, and arrow volleys pass over one at a time instead of becoming a steady hiss.
- You can follow one arrow volley over the rest: each passes in a short arc, every other one quieter. A hit on a body no longer sounds like dirt, a shield rings, and a volley is its hit or its patter, never both. Fewer impacts play at once, and other riders' misses are silent.
