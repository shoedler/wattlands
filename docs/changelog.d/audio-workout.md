### Added (Workout)

- Sound for the ride: chain tick and pedal press locked 1:1 to each rider's cranks on screen, a whirr and rattle by cadence and power, tyres and wind by speed, with gusts and patches of rough road so a steady block never freezes, a freewheel that ticks evenly and only when you coast, gear clicks with the chain crossing a quarter of a turn later, your breath on hard efforts. Other riders tick lightly around you, each on their own crank.
- A morning around you: wind in the grass that moves with the grass on screen, a busy dawn chorus that thins as the day comes up, near and far, a lark, a rooster, a far dog, a far church bell, a tractor while one crosses the screen.
- A crowd on scored sprints that claps and rings its cowbell on your cadence, and cheers at the finish. Never in the warm-up.

### Changed (audio)

- Safety prompts stay clear at the loudest moment of a ride, not just at a calm one: `warning` and `rescue` now push everything else down further and for longer, on every game.
- Workout's routine interval banner is silent: the warning sound means a real safety prompt again.
- Interface sounds step back under a safety prompt too, on every game; a start countdown and the metronome keep their level.
- A long pause no longer saves up the far sounds of the landscape to play them all at once when you ride on, on every game.
- A steady block moves more: stronger gusts and rough patches you hear in the tyres and the chain, a frame creak on the roughest road. The road, grass and birds repeat less, the dog and the church bell come round less often, and each revolution has a stronger and a weaker leg.
