### Added (Workout)

- Sound for the ride: chain tick and pedal press locked 1:1 to each rider's cranks on screen, a whirr and rattle by cadence and power, tyres and wind by speed, a freewheel that ticks only when you coast, gear clicks with the chain crossing a quarter of a revolution later, your breath on hard efforts. Other riders sound behind you as a quiet group.
- A morning around you: wind in the grass that moves with the grass on screen, a dawn chorus giving way to daytime birds, a lark, a rooster, a far dog, power lines, a tractor while one crosses the screen.
- A crowd on scored sprints that claps and rings its cowbell on your cadence, and cheers at the finish. Never in the warm-up.
