# Changelog: siege

### For riders

- **Castellum Rotae has a shared receipt.** In a break, picking an item from the Quartermaster no longer spends your gold on the spot. It goes on a receipt everyone in the room sees, with its cost, who picked it and the votes on it, and your gold for it is held ("40 ON RECEIPT" under your gold). Pick your own item again to take it back. Pick someone else's to vote no, and again to vote yes. If more riders vote no than yes, or the votes tie, the item comes off at once, the gold goes back to whoever picked it and everyone sees "Voted off". The row you light in the shop says what a surge will do: take back, vote no or undo your no. Upgrades stack as before: with level 1 on the receipt, level 2 shows as its own row with its own vote, and it goes if level 1 does. When the break ends, everything left on the receipt is bought, in the order it was added.
- **Pedalling alone no longer buys anything.** The shop's automatic pick in ERG is gone: the Autopilot still adds picks for a rider who does nothing, and they go on the receipt like anyone's, but a rider who only pedals can still scan the list and surge to pick.
- A rider who drops out changes nothing on the receipt: their items and votes stay until the break ends, and the break's end tells the team what the receipt cost.
