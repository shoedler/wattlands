# Changelog: siege

### For riders

- **Castellum Rotae has a shared receipt.** In a break, picking an item from the Quartermaster no longer spends your gold on the spot. It goes on a receipt everyone in the room sees, with its cost, who picked it and the votes on it, and your gold for it is held ("40 ON RECEIPT" under your gold). Pick your own item again to take it back. Pick someone else's to vote no, and again to vote yes. If more riders vote no than yes, or the votes tie, the item comes off at once and the gold goes back to whoever picked it. When the break ends, everything left on the receipt is bought, in the order it was added.
- **Pedalling alone no longer buys anything.** The shop's automatic pick in ERG is gone: the Autopilot still adds picks for a rider who does nothing, and they go on the receipt like anyone's, but a rider who only pedals can still scan the list and surge to pick.
- A rider who leaves the room takes their items off the receipt; their gold was never spent.
