# Decisions: picker cards

## 2026-10-31: every game's picker card is a render of the game itself

- Decision: Castellum Rotae, Diesel Domestiques and Turbo Reel each get a 480 x 270 card cut from a HUD-less 1920 x 1080 frame of the game's own view by one shared pair of scripts, `tools/picker_cards/render.sh` and `compose.mjs`. Workout keeps its 960 x 540 card, which its art bake makes and `bake.mjs --check` holds. The cuts reduce by whole factors: Tank by 2 (one texel per art pixel), Turbo Reel by 3 then doubled (two texels per art pixel), Castellum by 3 with the mean of each block, since its 2-pixel art does not divide by 3. Tank alone gets a shadow lift (black point 10, gamma 0.68), so the hull and tracks read and the sky stays night. Files are 256-colour PNGs. No card holds text, so a game's name can change.
- Composition: `AppGameCard` covers a slot 225 high and as wide as its grid column, 441 and 534 today, because a title wider than the 400 minimum widens the column. In the 534 slot only the middle 75 % of a card's height shows, so each subject fits inside that band.
- Turbo Reel's card moves to `assets/fishing/cards/` and leaves `FishingArtGrids`, `art.json` and `sprites.json`, since that set holds hand-authored grids only. `assets/tank/v3/tools_src/cards.sh` no longer renders a picker card; it keeps the content cards.
- Alternatives: 960 x 540 for all three (the same picture on screen under a nearest filter, larger files); a 1:1 art crop of Castellum (wheels twice as big, but no whole fortress and no moat); nearest instead of mean for Castellum (drops a third of its art pixels, so bricks shimmer); fal or any generated image (ruled out by the task).
- Reason: the owner asked for a set that reads at a glance from a trainer. The frames are not byte-reproducible, because lamps and water animate on wall-clock frames: a rerun gives the same composition, not the same bytes, so tests check aspect and loading, not pixels.
