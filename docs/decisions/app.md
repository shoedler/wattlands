# Decisions: app (pedal navigation, forms, Strava text, branding)

- **2026-10-24: Pedal navigation is opt-in.** The Settings toggle "Pedal navigation" (`RiderProfile.pedal_nav`, default off, replaces `pedal_ui`) is copied into `PlayerSlot.pedal_nav` for the local rider by the session launcher. Off, menus and in-game pick lists never scan, never auto-confirm and ignore surge and coast; on, or with Autopilot as the profile default, nothing changes. `InputRouter.pedal_nav_active()` decides for menus (and no longer counts "no device used yet"), `PedalScanList.pedal_nav` for lists. *Alternative:* keep pedal-scan on until a keyboard or controller is touched. *Reason:* a stray surge or a coast must never pick or back out for a rider who did not ask for pedal control.
- **2026-10-24: Zero-touch start and first-run creation stay.** Holding `pedal_start` on the title and the first run's 20 s of pedaling are start rules, not menu picks, and a new rider has no way to opt in first. Siege's Overdrive keeps its surge: it is gameplay, not a menu. Siege's shop follows `slot.pedal_nav`; its zero-input fallbacks (Autopilot picks and routing) cover a rider who neither pedals into a pick nor presses a key.
- **2026-10-24: One surge graph, under the page.** `WlSurgeGraph` shows the last 5 s of power sampled at 10 Hz and the threshold from `GestureRecognizer.surge_threshold_w()` (read-only, `max(1.3 x 10 s mean, mean + 40 W)`). Each app page owns one, shown with pedal navigation and hidden in ERG, where the surge reads flywheel speed. *Alternative:* one graph per list (a long list pushes it off screen).
- **2026-10-24: Every form scrolls its menu column.** `make_form_page` (Profile, Settings, first run) and the Rides, lobby and picker pages use `scroll_menu_column`, so the highlighted row, and the last rows, are always reachable by keyboard, controller and scan.
- **2026-10-24: The Strava text comes from the game's results.** `Rides` keeps the `GameResults` of the session until its ride is saved, writes `ride_tag`, `ride_lines`, the game title and the content title into the summary JSON (so a re-upload from the history has them), and `activity_name` and `activity_description` read the summary only. Title `WATTLANDS: <game> - <ride_tag>` (content title when the tag is empty, no " - " when both are); description: the game's lines, moving time, distance, average and normalized power, average cadence, work, "Recorded with WATTLANDS". No heart rate, no image (the Strava API takes none). The recorder's summary gains `np_w`.
- **2026-10-24: The product name shown to players is WATTLANDS.** Strings, the window title (`config/name`), FIT product name, Strava texts and doc prose. Identifiers, paths, `WATTLANDS_*` variables and the `Wl` prefix keep their spelling.
