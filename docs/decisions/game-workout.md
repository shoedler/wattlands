# Decisions: Workout (game-workout), W4 final pass

## 2026-10-21 (W4 final): A client's ride view interpolates the other riders and blends its own

- Decision: on a client, `WorkoutGame.apply_snapshot` records every rider's distance per snapshot in `WorkoutSnapHistory` (8 entries, keyed by the snapshot's tick × 0.05 s, the host's game time; a gap over 1.5 s starts it over). `WorkoutRideView.advance(delta)` (called from `_process`) runs a render clock on the frame delta, 0.1 s behind the newest snapshot (NetSession's documented `host_time() - 0.1`), catching up softly (τ 0.25 s) when a late snapshot held it, jumping only when 1.5 s off, and never passing the newest snapshot; the other riders are drawn linearly between the two snapshots around it. The local rider (and the camera, parallax and road with it) is drawn from its own prediction: it moves by its predicted speed each frame and blends toward the client state's distance (the host's latest plus `client_predict` since) with τ 0.3 s, never moves backwards, stops when the state has not moved for 0.15 s (a pause), and jumps only when the error passes 10 m. Rules, snapshots and the host/SOLO view are unchanged; the state still takes each snapshot in full.
- Alternatives: call `NetSession.snapshot_pair_at(host_time() - 0.1)` (a game has no NetSession handle in `GameContext`, and contracts are frozen); blend inside `WorkoutSnapshot.apply`/`WorkoutRules.predict` (changes the rules state and the snapshot tests that pin it; the error is only visual); keep snapping (BRIEF 5.1 asks for soft reconciliation).
- Reason: DoD finding confirmed[13], BRIEF 5.1: "clients predict their own rider locally and reconcile softly toward the host", and GAME_SDK 12's "interpolate everything else between snapshots". Before, a client's markers and background snapped to every 10 Hz snapshot.
