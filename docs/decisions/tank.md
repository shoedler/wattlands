## 2026-10-23 P0 simplifications (none touches BRIEF 4.6, 4.5, a plan shape, a directive pattern, a gate or a check)

- Decision, so a crew can play a full ashfall_road early:
  1. Enemy movement is kinematic: an enemy enters from its edge at `speed_px_s`, picks its hold distance once (from `hold_px` with `ctx.rng`), then keeps station with the hull (Advance) or stays fixed in world space (gun lines); lights weave sinusoidally by `weave_px`; no pathing and no enemy-enemy collision (the view draws overlaps on two depth rows). Destroyers hold at the screen edge and shift `move_after_shot_px` after each shot; heavies roll to their hold, telegraph 3 s, back off 30 px per 50 damage.
  2. Encounters come only from the mission's fixed lists, keyed by metres driven into the Advance (`at_m`); gun-line enemies spawn at the segment start. No random spawns.
  3. Hits: enemy hit rolls at fire time (the brief's rule) with the shell as a visual projectile that lands on the tank or 20-40 px off; friendly shells hit by projectile point against target boxes each tick, splash by distance at impact. The ground is flat at y 296 for ballistics (terrain grade moves only effort and speed); no occlusion.
  4. Boss: the brief's choreography (creep 2 px/s to 120 px, 24 px push-back per broken bogie, phase 2 immobile, main gun every 35-45 s / 25-35 s after a 6 s telegraph, secondary 3 every 5 s at 60%); auto-aim takes the frontmost standing bogie, then the turret.
  5. Depot drills are scripted: drive 0-120 s with no enemies, an aim drill on two drill wrecks 120-240 s (auto-aim slews to them; no damage, no score), a fire drill 240-300 s (each loaded shell fires at a drill wreck); one teaching toast per station at fixed times. The scripted drills add no effort beyond the depot's planned ramp and its directive patterns (terrain 0..+2%, boiler 3% x min(pressure, 1), Hoist 1%), and change no score.
  6. Manual controls are minimal but present: every brief 2.6 intent (lever, aim nudge 1 degree with the 5 s auto-aim pause, target cycle, traverse, fire, latch, ram, over) works through `local_intents`; the only on-screen control surface is the station card and the trajectory dots.
  7. Overruns: an Advance tow snaps the hull to the halt line at the next segment start (the fade is view-only); a gun-line support barrage removes the remaining gun-line enemies at the next segment start with no kill credit; a Behemoth withdrawal is `finished("withdrawn")` and the view shows it leaving during the cool-down.
  8. Breakdown repair counts the P_eff of whichever station drives (diesel or Bulwark), the reserve's 60 W included.
  9. Unlimited ammunition, one shell in the breech; the Rampart's hoist always lifts heavy shells automatically once the breech cycle is over; the Citadel's secondaries are automatic only.
- Stays exact: every BRIEF 4.6 and 4.5 rule (Tank only asks; the governor clamps), the segment table, the engine table, the rotation tables and the drill (2.3, 2.9), every directive pattern with its numbers (bank sub-steps, boiler, hoist, terrain at 2 Hz, cadence bands, haptics, end_phase), the in-band rules, every number in the 2.4 tables (HP, damage, gun timings, hit chances, energies, caps), the score, XP and unlock formulas, intent ownership and the 5/s limit, the 4 KB snapshot budget, the events, and every gate and check (T1-T6, S1-S8, static tests, conformance, e2e).
- Alternatives: full enemy AI with pathing and cover; a physics hit model with terrain occlusion; interactive depot drills.
- Reason: the brief's pillars are the workout and the cutaway machinery; these cuts remove work that changes neither, and none moves an effort target, a phase or a check.

## 2026-10-23 Who calls end_phase

- Decision: the rules (`TankRules.step`) set `state.seg.objective_done` (Advance: the halt line reached; gun line: every gun down; boss: the Behemoth dead) and `regroup` while min_s has not passed; the effort director (`TankDirector.step`, the only Tank code that calls `ctx.directives`) calls `end_phase(peer)` for every rider of the group's phase in one step once `objective_done` and the group's `elapsed_s >= min_s`, once per phase. The platform ends a phase at `max_s` on its own; the rules see that as `prev_ended_by == "max_s"` on the next phase and play the overrun. The module emits `finished(state.outcome)` once every rider is in the cool-down (`victory`, `withdrawn`, or `complete` for the drill, which has no boss).
- Alternatives: the module calls end_phase; the rules call it through a sink.
- Reason: one owner for every trainer-facing call keeps the no-bypass and directive checks in one place; the rules stay pure; all riders share boundaries, so one step ends them together.

## 2026-10-23 Steam 90% float tolerance

- Decision: steam "building" is `power_w >= 0.90 x FTP - max(1 W, 0.5% x FTP)` (`TankConst.steam_building`, the only source of the 90% and the tolerance; the duplicate `tanks.json` keys were removed). Never compare with float equality at 90%.
- Alternatives: exact `>= 90%`; a published steam power band (the mock would ride its centre).
- Reason: the SIM mock rides exactly 90% FTP with no band (notes, SDK 20) and power arrives in whole watts, so an exact comparison flips on rounding. The tolerance (1 W at FTP 200, 2 W at 400) is far below the 85% compliance gap (76.5% never builds), so T3 and T5 keep their meaning. The brief keeps the steam band off the power gauge, so no band is published for steam riders (the director clears it).

## 2026-10-23 Plan shape details

- Decision: one lap-true phase per mission segment for every rider (same kinds, lengths, min_s and max_s), modes and numbers from `stations.json` by the rider's engine in that segment; `PlanPhase.meta = {seg, stage, segment, engine, mission}`. The depot uses the slot's start engine; stage n uses the rotation table's column n and the boss column 4; a checkpoint already carries the next segment's engine (the crew moves at the checkpoint, so the station card shows the new engine during the 60 s at 50%). WARMUP-kind segments (the depot, the drill's skirmish) use the warm-up numbers for the boiler (3% x min(pressure, 1), never above +3% even if pressure overshoots within a tick; `TankDirector.boiler_grade_pct`), the hoist (1%) and terrain (0..+2%). The drill uses `stations.json` `drill_plans` (dynamo ERG 50 then 55 in `[55, 65]`) and ignores rotation.
- Bank (pattern 1): every target is `clamp(55 + 45 x (1 - bank/100), lo, hi)` of the rider's `plan.phase(i).effective_range()` (`stations.json` `bank.clamp_to_range`, `TankDirector.bank_target_pct`), clamped before the <= 10-point limit and the three 1 s sub-steps. It runs in every dynamo phase with a `range_pct`: the stages, the boss and the drill's WARMUP-kind skirmish (so 55..65 there, never an `outside_range_pct` refusal and never past the 65% warm-up cap); never in the depot or checkpoints (brief 2.3).
- In band follows the rider's own phase kind: every WARMUP and RECOVERY kind (the depot, the drill's skirmish, checkpoints) is cadence >= 60 only; the WORK rules (SIM 76-105% FTP, steam building >= 90%, ERG >= 95% of target) apply only in WORK phases (`TankRules.in_band`).
- Per-rider gates use the receiver's own phase: `TankRiderInput` carries `phase_kind`, `phase_mode`, `grade_source` and `plan_elapsed_s` from `frame.phase_of(peer)` and its plan, so the SIM-only patterns, haptics, the warm-up numbers and the first 300 s never read the group's phase (a rider can lag it by a tick).
- Checked (lead-owned `tests/games/tank/contracts/test_tank_plans.gd`): every crew x rotation on/off x content validates with no warning; the first 300 s stay within 4.6.8; per rider every ERG <-> SIM switch is >= 60 s apart with each open phase at its min_s and at its max_s; every segment's (kind, duration_s, min_s, max_s) equals the brief's table; the drill is 130 s with at most one switch per rider (cool-down included); Rampart and Citadel rotation rows per slot.
- Reason: brief 2.3's tables, with the checkpoint's engine the only free choice.

## 2026-10-24 G1 effort: patterns 1-6 and haptics

- Decision (director, `TankDirector`; every per-rider gate reads the receiver's own phase):
  1. Boiler (pattern 2): at most one send per `boiler.min_interval_s` (1 s), decreases included. The drop to 0 on a latch is one step sent at the first moment the spacing allows, so it can follow the latch by up to 1 s; the spacing carries across phase changes. Rise at most 0.5% per step; value 0 while the shell is loaded or cycling, `3% x min(p, 1)` in WARMUP kinds; sent only in the steam rider's SIM phases.
  2. Loader band (pattern 5): (60, 120) at every segment start, (50, 120) only while the steam rider's shell is loaded or cycling in a WORK phase (the in-band rule's loaded cadence of 50 applies only there), then back to (60, 120). WARMUP and RECOVERY kinds keep (60, 120).
  3. Hoist (pattern 3): the grade starts at 0 on reaching Hoist and rises `rise_pct_s x step_s` at each whole `hoist.step_s` (1, 2, 3, 4% at +1..+4 s), so it is never above 1%/s x time on Hoist; capped at 4% (1% in WARMUP kinds). A lever move (`move_s > 0` or `lever_to != Hoist`) counts as leaving: 0 at once. The ramp restarts from 0 at every phase change (the platform resets the channel). Nothing in the ERG gun line.
  4. Terrain (pattern 4): `course_grade` at 2 Hz (`terrain.send_every_s` 0.5) from the phase start, whether or not the value changed, in `grade_source` "game" phases only. The sampler is now the shared one (see "G2 integration" below).
  5. Bank (pattern 1): updates scheduled on ticks from the rider's own phase start (elapsed 0, 5, 10 s ...) with sub-steps at +0, +1 and +2 s; each update starts from the last accepted target (at the phase start, the plan's `planned_pct_at`), moves at most 10 points up or down, and sends nothing when goal equals from. "<= 3.3% FTP/s" in the brief is 10/3 rounded: tests assert <= 10/3 % per sub-step and <= 30 W/s at FTP 900.
  6. Haptics (2.7): exact table; hit and boss_hit to every rider in SIM, ram to the driver and taking precedence for them. Also skipped, to avoid a predictable `haptic_in_erg` refusal: riders on reserve (AFK or dropped) and riders whose applied mode is ERG inside a SIM phase. `haptics_on` is read from `PlayerSlot` (falling back to `TankRiderState`). The 15 s spacing compares with a 1e-6 s tolerance, so a haptic at exactly end + 15 s is sent. If the governor's cooldown or warm-up rule drops a haptic the host accepted, the director still counts it toward its own spacing: conservative, never more haptics.
  7. Bookkeeping (bank current, last grade, haptic end) counts only requests the sink accepted; a refused request is retried by the schedule.
- Alternatives: boiler decreases exempt from the 1 s spacing; a tempo power band in the warm-up; counting refused requests.
- Reason: the brief gives "once per second" and "decreases in one step" together; the spacing is the numeric rule a sim check can verify and the delay is far below T3's 6 s at grade 0. Every other choice is the conservative reading of BRIEF 4.6.6 and 4.6.8.

## 2026-10-24 G1 rules: engines, combat and drive models

- Decision:
  1. Host-only bookkeeping lives in `TankAux` (object metadata on `TankState`, `TankAux.of(state)`), so the frozen `TankState` is untouched; it is never serialized.
  2. Geometry is read only through `TankGeometry`. World frame: hull top y = ground 296 - 10 (skirt gap) - hull height; the Behemoth's y origin puts its bogies on the ground line. No occlusion (P0 ADR): shells pass through the boss hull body and concrete.
  3. Shells inherit the hull's velocity (world vx = muzzle velocity + 8 px/m x speed); stepping uses the exact arc (`x += vx dt`, `y += vy dt + g dt^2/2`), tested against the analytic arc; collisions sweep the segment against each hit box and take the earliest box or the ground. Enemy shells are straight tracers aimed at the tank centre (a hit) or the ground 20-40 px short of its nearer end (a miss), led for the hull's speed; the hit is rolled with the session RNG at fire time (hit chance x evasive 0.7 while the driver is in band), resolved when the flight ends, at once at the 24-shell cap.
  4. Barricades stop the tank `props.standoff_px` = 260 px short of them (where the gun can reach); a Ram charges the barricade it is stopped at; a light tank is rammable within 120 px reach + 24 px lunge of the hull's nearer end.
  5. Ram: `ability_1` / surge opens a 10 s window (SIM WORK phase, the rider's own plan time >= 300 s, cooldown 0). A target in reach with the sprint rule holding is destroyed (`ram ok 1`, kill BY_RAM); if a target was in reach and the rule never held, the window's close costs 10 HP (no hit event), a 5 s stall and a `ram ok 0`; a window with no target closes free. The 30 s cooldown starts when the window closes. In the boss segment a Ram is the lunge window only.
  6. Dodge: `dodge_s = min(sprint-rule held_s, seconds inside the telegraph)`; at 3 s the shot is marked dodged (event `dodge`, forced miss). The rule is a trailing 3 s average (about 1.75 s to hold from a 90% ride), so the sprint must start within about 1.25 s of the 6 s telegraph.
  7. Dynamo: +1 per 150 J of P_eff only while in band; drains the stabilizer (while an enemy is on screen or the boss stands), 0.5 per degree, 15 per traverse, 25 per Rampart hoist start, 2 per secondary shot (stopped below 10). A rescue freezes the bank, the turret and the hoist. The depot's bank is scripted to 100. The Rampart hoist starts only when a reachable target exists (or in the fire drill) and runs with the 6 s breech cycle.
  8. Steam: pressure from P_eff in any phase; at 100% a 5 s window (none in WARMUP phases or the rider's first 300 s: the shell latches at once); a latch intent inside the window or the Overpressure build gives a heavy shell now, from 40% a light one; checkpoints hold pressure; the breech cycle blocks building. Overpressure counts P_eff only while the sprint rule holds, bleeds 2%/s toward 100% otherwise, and latches at 150% or a heavy shell at 30 s.
  9. Bulwark: a lever move takes 1 s with no power; a manual lever intent turns auto off and it re-arms after 15 s; the auto-lever picks Hoist when unloaded with a reachable target, Turret when loaded and off target, else Drive; in an Advance, Drive when nothing stands within `auto_drive_clear_px`; a breakdown forces Drive; a rescue in a gun line zeroes the room's power. `drive_s` (T4) counts Drive time in the current Advance.
  10. Gun: auto-aim slews only once a shell is loaded (or in the depot aim drill) and fires within 0.5 degrees; priority tiers destroyer-in-telegraph, dug-in, heavy, medium (and idle destroyer), light, obstacle, nearer first; the manual target cycle walks on-screen targets left to right; a hand traverse pauses auto-aim 5 s like a nudge; the facing flips when the traverse completes. Citadel secondaries are hitscan (shot + hit events, no shell object).
  11. Rear light tanks cannot be reached by the main gun at all (24 px visible over the hull's rear); they are Ram prey and the Citadel's aft secondary shoots them. A raider with `linger_s` (light: 45 s) breaks off after that long (`EM_LEAVE`) and is removed off screen with no kill credit, so a compliant no-Ram crew stays alive.
  12. Behemoth: bogie index 0 is the one nearest the tank (rigs.json order; the contract's old "rear to front" wording was the opposite and is fixed); parts are engageable off screen; the turret is armoured in phase 1; it starts 470 px from the left edge so the Citadel does not begin inside the stop line.
  13. Objectives: Advance done at `length_m` (the tank stops there), a gun line when `guns_left` is 0 (which also clears the traps), the boss when the Behemoth is dead; fixed phases never. Overruns play at the next segment start (the P0 ADR's rules); barrage kills have slot -1 and no credit.
- Alternatives: serializing the bookkeeping; a physics hit model; an auto-aim that fires unloaded.
- Reason: the P0 simplifications of the G0 ADR, made concrete; every number stays in content.

## 2026-10-24 G1 module: Autopilot, prompts, snapshot, results

## 2026-10-24 G1 view: blur, lights, particles, interpolation, audio

- Decision:

## 2026-10-24 G4 effort: reserve riders, boss hold, end_phase retry, hit haptic (S1, S4, RN-5, ED-3)
