# Decisions: Workout (game-workout)

Short ADRs for the reference game Workout (`src/games/workout/`, `content/workout/`, `assets/workout/`, `tests/games/workout/`; BRIEF 3.7).

- **2026-10-21: Sessions are explicit phase lists in JSON, validated strictly at load.** Each content id has `content/workout/sessions/<id>.json` with every phase written out (no repeat syntax), or a `zwo` path. Unknown keys, a game-written COOLDOWN, `lap` other than true, a missing label, FREE phases in ERG and a missing 5-minute 50 → 65% warm-up ramp (unless `warmup_only`, which e2e uses) are rejected with one `push_error` listing every problem. *Alternatives:* a compact repeat syntax; building plans in code. *Reason:* the files read like BRIEF 3.7's table, the schema test compares them line by line, and a typo can't silently change a training plan.
- **2026-10-21: Easy 30's main set is 360 / 30 / 330 / 30 / 330 / 30 / 90 s.** The three FREE sprints start at minutes 6, 12 and 18 of the 20-minute main set and are their own SIM flat phases with laps. *Alternative:* sprints as directive SIM blocks inside one ERG phase. *Reason:* plan phases get FIT laps, the timeline shows them, the PlanValidator checks their switches, and Workout needs no directives at all.
- **2026-10-21: Workout sends no directives.** The plan does all the trainer work (ERG targets, SIM sprints, the course). No `cadence_band` either. *Reason:* the reference game should prove the plan path; every directive is a chance for a refusal the e2e gate would flag.
- **2026-10-21: In band.** ERG: power within ±5% of telemetry's `applied_target_w` (before the governor reports one, or while a ramped switch still has the trainer in SIM, the planned target × 0.85 for an unverified FTP). Rolling road (SIM WORK): power within the session band, 76–105% FTP. FREE sprints: cadence ≥ 60 rpm. Rescue, AFK and a dropped connection count as out of band and nothing else (a stagger). The warm-up counts toward the score; the platform's cool-down doesn't. *Alternative:* a few seconds of grace at each interval start. *Reason:* BRIEF 3.7 defines the band literally; the governor's slew and the mock's 2 s lag cost a compliant rider well under 10%.
- **2026-10-21: The group grade includes bots.** The group score is the average session time in band over every slot, so a solo ride with bots grades the whole group. Each row also carries its own grade in `stats`.
- **2026-10-21: Sprint scoring uses SprintRule's threshold with Workout's own streak counter.** A sprint is a hit once power stayed ≥ 1.2 × FTP for 3 s in a row; rows report the peak, the best streak and the time above. *Alternative:* `SprintRule.update/held_s/best_s`. *Reason:* SprintRule is a W1 stub here; `threshold_w()` already works, and window 0 (instantaneous) is exactly the per-step check, so the result is the same once SprintRule lands.
- **2026-10-21: Power bands on the gauge.** Rolling road shows 76–105% FTP; a FREE sprint after the first 300 s shows 120–160% FTP (MockTrainer's `erg` profile then rides the band centre, so bots sprint); a sprint inside the warm-up (e2e) shows none, so the rider stays easy there. Entering an ERG phase clears a band Workout set; the platform's cool-down clears the rest (OWNERSHIP 3.1 C27), so Workout sends nothing in the cool-down.
- **2026-10-21: Rolling road's course.** Three seeded sine waves (wavelengths 1.5–3, 3.5–7 and 8–15 km) sampled every 250 m over 50 km, normalized to exactly −2…+6% and rounded to 0.1%. The seed lives in the session file, so every rider and peer gets the same road. 50 km covers 30 minutes at 1000 W of P_eff on the steepest descent. *Alternative:* a random walk. *Reason:* smooth rolling hills, both limits reached, deterministic.
- **2026-10-21: Ride statistics.** Rows start from `GameResults.from_stats(ctx.stats, players)`; any value the platform reports as 0 is filled from Workout's own `WorkoutPowerStats` (kJ, average and normalized power, best 1/5/20-minute power from 1 Hz averages of the per-step telemetry). *Reason:* results stay correct whether or not the SessionStats tracker is wired, and tests can check them with synthetic frames.
- **2026-10-21: XP and progression.** XP = kJ + session time in band (%) + 10 per sprint hit. `ctx.progress` keeps totals (rides, kJ, XP, time in band, sprints hit), a level per 1000 XP, the best grade and cosmetic pennant unlocks (first ride, 5 rides, a grade S, 10 sprints, 5000 kJ); the newest unlock is the equipped pennant the view draws for the local rider.
- **2026-10-21: Snapshots are all ints and carry the timeline every 20th time.** Rows hold distance and course distance in decimetres, speed in cm/s, flags, time in band in permille, power and cadence; `int_fields` declares `t`, `p`, `pt`, `r[][]` and `tl[][]`. The timeline (`tl`, `tll`) goes out with the first snapshot and every 20th after it. *Reason:* a client can't always load the content itself (a `zwo:<path>` file exists only on the host), and a rejoining client gets only the latest snapshot.
- **2026-10-21: Imported .zwo phases are post-processed, then validated again.** Every phase gets `lap = true` and a label (`WORKOUT_LBL_BLOCK`, "Block n"), an imported Cooldown block becomes RECOVERY (the platform appends the real cool-down), and a SIM flat FreeRide becomes a FREE phase (cadence band, sprint scoring). The result goes through the same phase rules as a session file. Tests inject a parser while WorkoutImport is a W1 stub; `tests/games/workout/integration/` runs the real one.
- **2026-10-21: Workout's own parallax layer.** `WorkoutParallaxLayer` tiles a procedurally drawn texture and scrolls it by a world x the view sets. *Alternative:* world2d's `ParallaxStrip`. *Reason:* `ParallaxStrip` had no W0 stub to code against, so a reference would not parse in W1; the swap is a small change once world-kits lands.
- **2026-10-21: on_rescue and on_afk hold until their false call (hardening review).** The callbacks set flags on the rider that `WorkoutRules` ORs with telemetry's `rescue`, `afk` and `connected`, so a rider the platform marks AFK (a drop, a rejoin before fresh pedaling) or rescued is out of band and drawn that way until the platform clears it, even if telemetry doesn't say so yet. *Alternative:* telemetry flags only (the first build overwrote the callback's effect on the next step). *Reason:* BRIEF 3.6 makes the callbacks the platform's word on staggers; nothing but time in band is taken away either way.
- **2026-10-21: In Rolling road the ride view moves riders on the trainer's applied SIM grade (hardening review).** `WorkoutRules` integrates each rider's `SpeedModel` on telemetry's `applied_grade_pct` while the trainer reports SIM in a course phase (the course grade under the rider only until then), and counts course distance from the phase start. *Alternative:* the raw course grade at Workout's own distance (the first build). *Reason:* the rider's own speed model follows `course_profile` by P_eff on the applied grade (difficulty-scaled, gears included); on the raw grade Workout's riders fell kilometres behind over 30 minutes of climbing, so the hill on screen no longer matched the grade on the trainer.
- **2026-10-21: Imported block labels are plain text.** "Block n" comes from `WORKOUT_LBL_BLOCK` when the strings table is registered and from a literal fallback otherwise (a headless run), never the bare key, because the label is also the FIT lap name.
- **2026-10-21: Ride view scales.** Riders and the course profile at 2 m per art pixel (the local rider at x 200), the course elevation exaggerated 2.5 px per metre, parallax at 3 px per metre at factor 1, and the interval blocks at 0.5 px per plan second (about 21 minutes across the screen). Pennants are polygon lists drawn at 1.5 × their base size (pole heights staggered by slot so a bunch stays readable), and the picker card rasterizes the same shapes at 2 ×. `src/games/workout/tools/render_view.gd` renders the view from a snapshot fixture (windowed) for a look at the art.
- **2026-10-21 (W2): The ride view uses world2d's ParallaxStrip; sprints use the real SprintRule.** Supersedes "Workout's own parallax layer" and "Sprint scoring uses SprintRule's threshold with Workout's own streak counter". The five backdrop strips are `ParallaxStrip`s (top edge at the old `top_y`, `cover_width` 640, no drift, scroll snapped to whole art pixels) and `WorkoutParallaxLayer` is gone; `WorkoutSprintTracker` feeds `SprintRule` (window 0) on the sprint's own clock and reads `held_s()`/`best_s()`, keeping its own total of time held. *Alternative:* keep Workout's stand-ins. *Reason:* both platform classes exist now (B 3.9, SH1); the view scrolls the same way (rounded instead of floored, under one art pixel) and the sprint results are unchanged (the tests that pinned them pass as they are).

## 2026-10-21 (W4): Rolling road normalizes its course over an 8 km window

- Decision: `WorkoutCourse` takes an optional `window_m` (a multiple of `step_m`, at most `length_m`). The road is generated over [0, window_m], normalized to exactly span the session's grades there, and repeated back and forth (mirrored, so no grade jumps at a seam) up to `length_m`. Rolling road uses 8 km, which even a 120 W P_eff rider passes in the 30-minute phase.
- Alternatives: shorter wavelengths over 50 km (the extremes still land wherever the waves put them); repeat the window without mirroring (a grade jump at every seam).
- Reason: W3 WO-3: normalized over 50 km, the first -2 % point lay at 36 km, beyond every rider's 30 minutes, so the road ridden spanned about -1..+6 %.

## 2026-10-21 (W4): Only a FREE phase of at most 30 s is a sprint

- Decision: `WorkoutSession.is_sprint(phase)` is a FREE phase of at most 30 s (BRIEF 3.7's sprints). Only sprints get the 120-160 % band, the sprint banner and countdown and SprintRule scoring. A longer FREE phase (an imported FreeRide) is free riding: no band, `WORKOUT_BANNER_FREE`, no sprint result; its time in band stays cadence >= 60 rpm.
- Alternatives: a `sprint` flag in session files (a .zwo has no such flag); a 60 s limit (a 60 s FreeRide is not a sprint either).
- Reason: W3 WO-6: a 10-minute FreeRide told the rider to hold 120-160 % FTP for 10 minutes.

## 2026-10-21 (W4): Imported ERG blocks below the floor ride the floor

- Decision: `WorkoutSession.from_zwo` raises an imported ERG block (target or ramp end) below 30 % FTP to 30 % and lists it with the importer's clamps (`WORKOUT_CLAMP_FLOOR`) instead of rejecting the file.
- Alternatives: accept 0 % in Workout's own re-check and leave it to the governor's floor (the preview would show a 0 % block the rider never rides).
- Reason: W3 WO-5 and BRIEF 3.7 ("clamps each block to 4.6 ... listing each clamp"): a single `Power="0"` rest rejected the whole workout. A floor clamp in the importer itself belongs to its owner.

## 2026-10-21 (W4): A long timeline goes in snapshot parts; results show best powers

- Decision: a timeline of more than 120 phases travels in parts (`tlo`, `tln`) on consecutive snapshots at the start of each 20-snapshot cycle; a client sets its timeline once every part is in. `WorkoutResults` adds the best 1-, 5- and 20-minute power (only for windows the ride covered) and every sprint's peak to each row's display stats.
- Reason: W3 NET-3 (a 400-block .zwo pushed the timeline snapshot past 16 KB) and WO-2 (BRIEF 3.7 lists those results; the results screen draws only the row's stats beyond its fixed columns).
