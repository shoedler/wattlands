# Decisions: sim (SimClock, SeededRng, SpeedModel, WBalance, Bots, GestureRecognizer, SprintRule, WorkoutImport)

Short ADRs for `addons/wattlands/sim/` (BRIEF 3.3-3.7, 3.11, 4.6.1, 4.6.8).

- **2026-10-21: "For N s" in gestures is timed from the first sample of the stretch.** pedal_start (3 s), coast (3 s) and stop_5s (5 s) fire on the first sample at least N s after the stretch's first sample; the optional `advance(t)` fires them on time between samples, holding the latest sample's state for at most 2.5 s. *Alternatives:* counting samples; timing from the latest sample only. *Reason:* the governor's coasting rule (4.6.4, BRIEF 7.4) is timed the same way, so the gesture and the governor agree; `advance` keeps the ~1 Hz jitter out of the timing for callers that tick.
- **2026-10-21: A surge is two consecutive qualifying samples against a frozen 10 s baseline.** "For 1.5 s" means two consecutive ~1 Hz samples at least 0.5 s apart (a duplicate notification never counts twice). Both compare against the mean of the samples in the 10 s before the streak's first sample (power outside ERG, flywheel speed in ERG), so the surge never raises its own bar. One surge per streak; a sample that doesn't qualify re-arms it. *Alternatives:* a rolling average that includes the surge (a marginal 1.3 x surge then fails on its second sample); a time-weighted average. *Reason:* the threshold the brief states holds for the whole 1.5 s, and the sample mean equals the time-weighted mean at ~1 Hz.
- **2026-10-21: Starting to pedal is never a surge.** The surge baseline counts only samples after the latest not_pedaling sample and needs 5 s of that history, and a surge needs pedal_start to have fired since the last stop. *Alternatives:* include stopped samples (a restart from 0 W then looks like +40 W); no minimum. *Reason:* pedal-scan menus would otherwise pick on every restart.
- **2026-10-21: coast and stop_5s are used up per stop where they aren't allowed.** A stop that reaches 3 s (5 s) during ERG (or, for stop_5s, in multiplayer) never fires that gesture later in the same stop, even if ERG ends. *Alternative:* fire as soon as the stop is outside ERG. *Reason:* a "back" arriving seconds after a phase change would surprise the rider; the arbitration in the W0 stub says each fires once, at its time, where allowed.
- **2026-10-21: not_pedaling is a state, not a fired gesture.** `not_pedaling()`, `not_pedaling_s()`, `not_pedaling_s_at(t)`, `not_pedaling_since()` and the `not_pedaling_changed` signal expose it; `on_sample` only returns `Wl.GESTURES`. *Reason:* GM8 lists the gestures games get as pedal_start, surge, coast and stop_5s; not_pedaling feeds the governor and coast/stop_5s.
- **2026-10-21: SprintRule treats each update as the power since the previous one.** A 1 Hz sample at t covers (t - 1, t]; `held_s()` runs from the start of the first holding update's interval; with a window, `holds()` needs updates covering the whole window and compares its time-weighted average. *Alternatives:* forward hold; held_s from the first holding update. *Reason:* the same reading serves sim-step feeds and 1 Hz samples, and "over the last 3 s" (Tank's Ram) then means exactly 3 s of data.
- **2026-10-21: The importer keeps a .zwo's own warm-up.** A prefix (300 s ERG 50 -> 65%) goes first unless the first block is a Warmup or every ERG phase starting in the first 300 s already stays <= 65%. An ERG phase that starts inside 300 s and has a value above 65% is split at 300 s (the second part continues the lap), then the inside part is clamped to 65%. *Alternatives:* always prefix (two warm-ups); clamp the whole crossing block to 65% (a 10-minute warm-up ramp loses its top half). *Reason:* riders import workouts with warm-ups; the split keeps the file's ramp where 4.6.8 allows it, and the PlanValidator decides warm-up membership by phase start.
- **2026-10-21: Short ERG/SIM runs are extended to 30 s.** Any run of SIM (or ERG) phases between two switches shorter than 30 s gets its last phase lengthened to 30 s; the appended ERG cool-down counts as a switch after a final SIM run. *Alternatives:* merge a short FreeRide into its ERG neighbours or convert a short ERG gap to SIM. *Reason:* extending keeps every block's mode and target and changes the session length by seconds; the PlanValidator then has no spacing warning to report.
- **2026-10-21: Duration limits are checked per phase by its peak, and the whole offending phase is clamped.** A phase whose peak is above 120% (105%) accrues its full length; >= 120 s of consecutive ERG phases with peaks <= 90% reset both counts (inclusive); a SIM phase restarts that count. A phase that would push the 120% count past 60 s is clamped to 120%, then one that would push the 105% count past 300 s to 105%; exactly 60 s and 300 s are allowed. *Alternatives:* split the offending block at the limit; integrate ramps exactly. *Reason:* it is never less strict than the PlanValidator's reading (PL17: accrued at the phase's range top), so an imported plan is never rejected, and "clamp the offending block" is what the session preview lists.
- **2026-10-21: Rejected .zwo files use push_warning.** `parse_zwo` returns `ok: false` with `error` for empty, non-XML, wrongly rooted, truncated or blockless files and logs it with `push_warning`. *Alternative:* `push_error`. *Reason:* a .zwo is user content, so a bad one is an expected, non-fatal condition like a refused directive; push_error would make every game test that imports a bad file need `assert_push_error`.
- **2026-10-21: Phase kinds come from the file's values.** Kinds follow the tag and the block's own power (SteadyState >= 76% WORK, Ramp by its upper end), before any clamp, so a block clamped in the warm-up keeps its kind. *Reason:* the kind describes the coach's intent; the target shows the clamp.
- **2026-10-21: Importer text is translated from content/strings/sim.csv.** Phase labels ("Steady 88%", "Interval 2/5 110%"), clamp lines and warnings are built from `SIM_ZWO_*` keys with `{named}` placeholders at parse time; labels show the clamped values. *Reason:* labels are plain text the HUD and FIT laps show as-is (PL11), and platform strings live in the module's CSV.
- **2026-10-21: XML details.** Godot 4.7's XMLParser decodes entities (named and numeric) in text and attribute values itself and keeps CDATA content in `get_node_name()` (`get_node_data()` errors on CDATA), so the importer adds no decoding. Tag and attribute names match case-insensitively; a power above 5.0 is read as a percentage with a warning; IntervalsT without OnPower/OffPower uses the mean of PowerOnLow/PowerOnHigh (PowerOffLow/PowerOffHigh) with a warning.
- **2026-10-21: A part split off at 300 s keeps its block's length for the long-phase cap.** When the warm-up split cuts a block longer than 300 s into a part inside the warm-up and a part after it, the 105% cap for phases longer than 300 s still applies to the second part, even if that part alone is 300 s or shorter. *Alternative:* judge each part by its own length (the PlanValidator would accept that too). *Reason:* the split exists only to keep a file's warm-up within 4.6.8; it must not lift the cap the block had as written ("the 105% cap for blocks longer than 300 s").
- **2026-10-21: Guards against malformed .zwo files.** A leading byte-order mark is ignored; an IntervalsT `Repeat` above 200 is limited to 200 with a warning; a workout whose parsed blocks add up to more than 24 h is rejected (`ok: false`); a missing file returns the full result shape with a translated error and the same `push_warning` as any rejected file. *Alternatives:* no limits; skipping the oversized block. *Reason:* a .zwo is user content, and a typo like `Repeat="10000"` would otherwise build tens of thousands of phases on the host and on every client.
- **2026-10-21: Importer and gesture tests include seeded property sweeps.** 300 random .zwo files must import, satisfy an independent reading of the validator's rules, never raise any block's target, keep laps and import identically twice; 120 random gesture streams in each ERG/solo combination must fire only what BRIEF 3.5 allows, and every stop exactly what it should. A PlanValidator test over the fixtures and 100 random files is `pending` while PlanValidator is the W0 stub, and becomes a real check (no error, no warning, only the cool-down appended) once sdk-runtime's validator is merged. *Reason:* the fixtures pin each rule; the sweeps catch interactions between the importer's passes and between gesture rules.
- **2026-10-21: .zwo durations are whole seconds.** `Duration`, `OnDuration` and `OffDuration` are rounded to the nearest second; one that rounds to 0 makes the block invalid (skipped with a warning), and an `OffDuration` that rounds to 0 gives on phases only. *Alternative:* keep fractions. *Reason:* Zwift writes whole seconds, converters leave artefacts like 29.999999 that would otherwise trigger a spurious 30 s spacing clamp, and a sub-tick phase (under 0.05 s) would end inside a single sim step.
