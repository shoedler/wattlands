# Credits

Third-party code, fonts, audio and generated art used by Wattlands. Platform entries are merged here from `docs/credits.d/` fragments by the lead; games added after v0.1.0 keep their entries in `docs/credits.d/<id>.md`. Dependency details (versions, sources, SHA-256 sums) are in `docs/DEPENDENCIES.md` and `docs/deps.d/`.

## Code

- GUT 9.7.1 (Godot Unit Test), MIT, https://github.com/bitwes/Gut (tests only).
- GDBLE v0.6.0, MIT, https://github.com/Fantety/GDBLE (Bluetooth LE GDExtension on btleplug).
